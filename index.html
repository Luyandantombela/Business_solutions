About

Why businesses work with me

Most business software problems aren't hard because of the code. They're hard because nobody has taken the time to actually understand the process, find where it breaks, and design something that fits how the business really works.

That's the part I enjoy. I sit with the messy spreadsheet, the manual checklist, the report someone rebuilds from scratch every Friday — and I turn it into a system that runs itself, reliably, without needing a technical person to babysit it.

I'm not selling hours of programming. I'm selling the outcome: fewer errors, faster turnaround, and time back for the people on your team who were doing this work by hand.
